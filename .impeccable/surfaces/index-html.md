## Direction contract

THESIS: One imprint sheet for a small software press: the studio name, then titles with their place of publication. Refuses a centered headline over equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white sheet on a cooler gray desk, carbon ink, one deep steel-blue accent only on the live title’s register mark and its link. Hairline rules, a soft offset shadow under the sheet, no pills. Schibsted Grotesk. Entries are full-width imprint rows.
